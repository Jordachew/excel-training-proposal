Engagement Brief

From spreadsheet users to stronger business analysts.

This proposal is structured around practical Excel application, analytical reasoning, reporting discipline, and repeatable workflows for professionals who rely on Excel daily.

Training Positioning

Advanced Excel for Business Analysis, Reporting, and Decision Support is designed for analysts and finance professionals who need to move beyond basic formulas into structured analysis, modelling, dashboards, automation, and insight generation.

The course will use practical business scenarios relevant to FP&A, sales, accounting, production, inventory, budget, forecast, and variance analysis workflows.

Course Content

2-Day Advanced Microsoft Excel Curriculum.

A practical curriculum combining technical skill development, applied business analysis, and guided exercises.

Day 1

Data, formulas, and analytical foundations
Module 1: Advanced Excel Mindset Workbook structure, model discipline, inputs, calculations, outputs, and checks.
Module 2: Data Structure & Cleaning Excel Tables, validation, duplicates, date/text cleanup, and data quality checks.
Module 3: Advanced Formulas For Analysts XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, IFS, dynamic arrays, text functions, and error handling.
Module 4: PivotTables & Analytical Reporting PivotTables, grouping, slicers, PivotCharts, calculated fields, and common pitfalls.
Module 5: Introduction To Power Query Import, clean, transform, merge, append, and refresh reporting data.

Day 2

Modelling, dashboards, controls, and business reasoning
Module 6: FP&A And Business Modelling Forecast logic, assumptions, revenue/cost drivers, and structured model design.
Module 7: Variance & Performance Analysis Actual vs budget, actual vs prior year, price-volume-mix thinking, and commentary.
Module 8: Scenario & Sensitivity Analysis Goal Seek, Data Tables, scenario analysis, downside/base/upside cases.
Module 9: Dashboards & Management Reporting KPI selection, layout, slicers, charts, conditional formatting, and decision-ready views.
Module 10-11: Controls And Capstone Case Audit checks, reconciliation, formula review, and a full business case buildout.
