Instructor – Jordache Wilson
Jordache Wilson is the Group Finance Planning and Analysis Manager at Jamaica Public Service (JPS), with over a decade of experience in financial planning, performance management, and data-driven decision-making. He has held senior roles at leading organizations including Digicel Jamaica and Red Stripe/Heineken, where he led budgeting and forecasting processes, developed financial models, and delivered insights that strengthened business performance.
Beyond his corporate career, Jordache has trained and worked with a wide range of organizations in professional and instructional capacities, including Seprod, the University of the West Indies (UWI), Red Stripe, Caribbean Broilers (CBL), and numerous small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). As Managing Director of JW Business Solutions, he delivers practical, hands-on training in Advanced Microsoft Excel and data analytics, equipping professionals with real-world skills in automation, financial modeling, and data-driven decision-making.
Co-presenter - Kadejha Colquhoun
Kadejha Colquhoun is a valuation professional based in Kingston, Jamaica, currently serving as a Senior Associate at PwC Jamaica within the Deal Advisory practice. She possesses over five years of experience in business valuation, supported by three years of accounting experience, which together underpin her strong technical capabilities in financial analysis and modeling. Kadejha has contributed to a range of complex valuation engagements for both government and private sector clients, with industry exposure spanning food and beverage, energy, and transportation etc.
She demonstrates advanced proficiency in Excel, including the development of complex, dynamic financial models, application of Power Pivot for large data sets, and the use of automation techniques to enhance analytical efficiency and reporting accuracy.
